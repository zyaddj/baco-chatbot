# Employee Handbook - Test Document

## Company Policies

### Dress Code
Employees are expected to dress professionally during business hours. Business casual attire is acceptable Monday through Thursday. On Fridays, casual dress including jeans is permitted, but clothing should still be neat and appropriate for a workplace environment.

### Work Hours
Standard business hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Flexible work arrangements may be available with manager approval.

### Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with prior approval from their direct supervisor. Remote work days must be scheduled in advance.

### Vacation Policy
All full-time employees accrue vacation time at a rate of 15 days per year during their first two years of employment. After two years, the accrual rate increases to 20 days per year.

### Safety Guidelines
- Always wear safety equipment in designated areas
- Report any safety hazards immediately to your supervisor
- Emergency exits are located at the front and rear of each floor
- In case of fire, use stairs only - never use elevators

### IT Security
- Passwords must be at least 8 characters long and include numbers
- Do not share login credentials with anyone
- Report suspected security breaches immediately
- All company data must be backed up regularly

### Meeting Etiquette
- Arrive on time for all scheduled meetings
- Come prepared with necessary materials
- Respect speaking time limits
- Follow up with action items within 24 hours
